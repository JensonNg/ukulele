    Tips for your uke

    New strings stretch for a week or two, so tune before every song. If the note rises as you turn, you are tightening. Small turns, then pluck again.

    Play-along songs

    One bar of clicks counts you in. The big card is the chord to play now; the small one is coming next.

    Singing while you play

    1. Learn the chord changes with Backing uke on until your hands move without thinking.
    2. Turn Backing uke off and play one down strum per bar while you hum the tune.
    3. Find your first note: strum the first chord, then hum until your voice blends with it.
    4. Sing the words. If your strumming stops, drop back to one strum per bar; the voice leads, the uke follows.
